Flask-Exts Demo

A small reference app for admin views, inline editing, filters, AJAX lookups, exports, and object-level authorization.

Demo accounts

Username Password Role
admin admin Admin

Local demo only. Do not reuse these credentials in production.

What to try

  • After signing in as admin, open Posts for search, multi-column sorting, custom filters, and CSV/XLS export.
  • Edit authors and posts, and try inline editing in the list view.
  • Sign in as alice or bob to see Interests limited to the rows they own.
  • Use the language selector in the navbar to switch English and Chinese.

Notes

  • Data lives in demo.sqlite and is rebuilt from sample data when missing.
  • The demo uses SECRET_KEY="demo" and skips production hardening.
